First draft

Objectives

Explain what photosynthesis is. Name what a plant takes in and gives out.

Starter activity · 5 min

Show two plants, one kept in light and one in the dark. Ask: what changed?

Main activity · 30 min

Short talk with a leaf diagram. Groups build the word equation with cards: carbon dioxide + water, with sunlight, gives glucose + oxygen.

Quick quiz · 10 min

1. Which gas do plants take in? 2. Where in the leaf does it happen? 3. What does chlorophyll do?

Use AI the right way

AI should support teachers, not replace their judgment.

You stay in charge

AI gives the first draft. You decide what is right for your class. Your judgment comes first.

Check before you use

Teachers must check generated facts, questions, feedback and research citations. AI can sound sure and still be wrong.

Protect student data

Student data should not be uploaded into public AI tools without proper permission and safeguards. In our session we use sample data only.
