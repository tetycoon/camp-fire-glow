Myths vs truth

3 myths about AI at work

Myth

AI is only for programmers.

Truth: Most AI tools work with plain sentences. If you can write a message, you can use them.

Myth

AI does the whole job for you.

Truth: AI makes the first draft. You decide, edit and own the final result.

Myth

AI is always right.

Truth: AI can sound sure and still be wrong. You will learn how to check it.
